## What is deleted

Your name, email and password hash, UPI ID, accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring entries, savings goals, group memberships, shared expenses you paid, your splits, settlements to or from you, and invite links you created.

## What other people keep

Groups you were in stay for the other members. Shared expenses you paid and payments involving you are removed from those groups, so their group balances may change. Entries that were already added to other members' own trackers stay with them. Consider settling up and telling your group before you go.

## Before you delete

Download your transactions from Finance as a CSV file if you want a copy. Settle any open balances with friends.

## Backups

Our database provider keeps a short recovery history of up to {site.hosting.backupWindow}. Deleted data disappears from it when that window passes.

## Cannot log in?

Email <EmailLink email={site.privacyEmail} subject="Delete my account" /> from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm it is you and process the request within 30 days, following the same timeline.
